Why I built Tracknrent

This isn't a startup pitch. It's a story about a business I lost.

I ran a rental company. Chairs, tables, canopies, the usual. I thought I was organised because I had a notebook and a WhatsApp group with my staff.

Then I lost a booking. Then two. A bride's family called me the night before her wedding because half the chairs they'd paid for were still at another venue. I had written "returned" next to them in my book. My staff had written "still out" in the WhatsApp group. Neither of us was lying. We just had no single source of truth.

That was the beginning of the end. The refunds, the angry calls, the reviews. I didn't lose the business because I was bad at renting things. I lost it because I couldn't keep track of what I owned and who had it.

So I built Tracknrent. First for myself — to never make that mistake again. Then I realised every rental business in Nigeria has the same problem. We're all running multi-million naira operations on notebooks and memory.

Tracknrent is the tool I wish someone had given me before I lost everything.

Questions people ask me

Who is this for?

Anyone who rents out physical items for events. Chair and table rental companies, tent companies, party planners, DJs with equipment, inflatable rental, lighting and sound. If you own things and rent them out, Tracknrent works.

I've never used software like this. Is it hard?

No. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use Tracknrent. Add your items, make a booking, send a receipt. That's it. I built it for people who run their business from their phone, not for IT departments.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Tracknrent is built mobile-first. It works in any browser on any phone. You can also install it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

What if I already have bookings in a notebook?

Just add your items first, then create bookings as they come. You don't have to migrate everything at once. Start with what you have, grow from there.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. You can invite team members. They can manage bookings and inventory. You decide what they can see and do. Only the owner can delete bookings or take the store offline.

Why ₦6,000 for 4 months?

I priced it so one lost booking pays for it four times over. If Tracknrent prevents a single double-booking, it's already paid for itself. If it prevents two, you're in profit.

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for event rentals?

Yes, that's our focus. Tracknrent is optimised for chairs, tables, tents, and party equipment — the exact categories event rental businesses deal with every weekend. That said, any rental business with quantity-based inventory will find it fits.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses add their inventory and create their first booking in under 5 minutes. Sign up, list your items with quantities and prices, and you're ready to book.

Can I track quantities (e.g., 200 chairs)?

Yes — quantities are the whole point. Add "Chiavari Chair — 200 units" once, and Tracknrent tracks total owned, currently reserved, and free for any date you pick.

What happens if two bookings overlap?

Tracknrent warns you before you save. If you choose to proceed anyway (say, you're borrowing from a vendor), you record the vendor's name on the booking and the system keeps a clear trail. Nothing gets silently lost.

Can I send receipts on WhatsApp?

Yes — one tap generates a clean receipt and opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled. You can also download it as a PDF or image.

Do my team members get access?

Yes. Invite partners or staff as team members. They can manage bookings, inventory, and your public page — with owner-only controls for sensitive actions.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no lock-in. If Tracknrent isn't saving you time, cancel and walk away.

I ran a rental company. Chairs, tables, canopies, the usual. I thought I was organised because I had a notebook and a WhatsApp group with my staff.

Then I lost a booking. Then two. A bride's family called me the night before her wedding because half the chairs they'd paid for were still at another venue. I had written "returned" next to them in my book. My staff had written "still out" in the WhatsApp group. Neither of us was lying. We just had no single source of truth.

That was the beginning of the end. The refunds, the angry calls, the reviews. I didn't lose the business because I was bad at renting things. I lost it because I couldn't keep track of what I owned and who had it.

So I built Tracknrent. First for myself — to never make that mistake again. Then I realised every rental business in Nigeria has the same problem. We're all running multi-million naira operations on notebooks and memory.

Tracknrent is the tool I wish someone had given me before I lost everything.

Questions people ask me

Who is this for?

Anyone who rents out physical items for events. Chair and table rental companies, tent companies, party planners, DJs with equipment, inflatable rental, lighting and sound. If you own things and rent them out, Tracknrent works.

I've never used software like this. Is it hard?

No. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use Tracknrent. Add your items, make a booking, send a receipt. That's it. I built it for people who run their business from their phone, not for IT departments.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Tracknrent is built mobile-first. It works in any browser on any phone. You can also install it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

What if I already have bookings in a notebook?

Just add your items first, then create bookings as they come. You don't have to migrate everything at once. Start with what you have, grow from there.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. You can invite team members. They can manage bookings and inventory. You decide what they can see and do. Only the owner can delete bookings or take the store offline.

Why ₦6,000 for 4 months?

I priced it so one lost booking pays for it four times over. If Tracknrent prevents a single double-booking, it's already paid for itself. If it prevents two, you're in profit.
