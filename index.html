Questions? Answers.

The things rental owners ask us most.

Do I need internet for it to work?

Any phone with internet works. Your data syncs live so multiple staff can use it at the same time.

Can I track items I borrow from vendors?

Yes. When a booking needs more items than you own, Tracknrent flags the shortage and lets you record which vendor you're borrowing from — with return deadlines and overdue alerts.

What happens if an item comes back damaged?

Log damaged items with quantities and charges. Inventory adjusts automatically so future bookings stay accurate.

Do my staff get access too?

Yes. Invite team members with Owner or Partner roles. Partners can create and edit bookings but can't delete or unpublish your storefront.

Can customers see my catalog online?

Every business gets a shareable storefront with your catalog, gallery, and contact info. Share the link on WhatsApp, Instagram, or business cards.

How much does it cost?

₦6,000 for 4 months of full access. No per-item limits, no commission on bookings.
